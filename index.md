## The short version
BinFinder keeps your lists and photos on your phone. It has no account system, no ads, and no servers of its own. We do not collect, sell or share your personal information.

## What the app stores
- **Bins and items:** names, locations, categories, quantities, notes and values that you type or speak.
- **Photos:** pictures you take of your items, saved in the app's private storage on your phone.
- **Object Finder locations (optional):** the GPS coordinates of your phone when you save a photo in Object Finder, so you can find the object again. Saved only on your device.
- **AI descriptions and labels:** text produced from your photos and saved with the item so you can search it.

All of this stays on your device. It is not sent to us.

## Permissions
- **Camera:** to photograph items, scan QR codes and show the live viewfinder. Photos are only saved when you take them.
- **Location (optional):** in Object Finder, the app reads your phone's location when you save a photo and stores the coordinates with that object. If you deny the permission, objects are saved without coordinates and everything else works. Coordinates are not sent anywhere.
- **Microphone (through your phone's voice typing):** when you tap a Speak button, your phone's own speech-recognition service opens and returns the words to the app. BinFinder does not record audio itself. Your phone's voice service, such as Google's, handles speech under its own privacy policy.

## On-device AI and Google services
BinFinder uses Google ML Kit and Gemini Nano to recognize objects and describe photos. This processing happens on your phone, so your photos are not uploaded to us or to Google for recognition. Google may collect limited usage and diagnostic data from ML Kit, as described in [Google's ML Kit terms and privacy information](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms). Google's own policies govern that data.

## Backups, printing and sharing
- **Backup file:** when you choose "Back up to file", the app creates one file containing your data and photos and saves it where you choose, such as Google Drive. You decide where it goes.
- **Android backup:** Android may back up the app's data to your own Google account if you have device backup turned on. That is controlled by your phone's settings.
- **Printing and sharing:** QR labels and bin lists go only to the printer, app or person you pick.

## Children
BinFinder is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Your choices
You can edit or delete any item or bin inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes its stored data from your phone. Backup files you created yourself must be deleted by you.
